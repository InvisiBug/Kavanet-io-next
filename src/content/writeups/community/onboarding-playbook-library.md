# Onboarding Playbook Library

## Overview
Onboarding Playbook Library is a set of practical guides for helping newcomers contribute to active community projects.

## Contents
- First-week contribution checklists
- Role-based quick starts
- Communication and review norms

## Why It Matters
Good onboarding improves retention and helps contributors make meaningful contributions sooner.

## Outcome
Teams adopting these playbooks reported faster onboarding and fewer repeated setup questions.
