# EJC Clubs 2024

## Date

## Overview
EJC Clubs 2024 was a community-focused program of clubs and creative tech sessions designed to help participants build confidence through making.

## Highlights
- Weekly themed sessions
- Hands-on workshops with collaborative builds
- Public showcase of participant projects
