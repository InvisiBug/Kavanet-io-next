# Flowception Dome Slackfest 2025

## Date
2025-08-01

## Overview
Flowception Dome Slackfest 2025 explored immersive visuals, responsive lighting, and participatory controls in a shared dome experience.

## Highlights
- Live-reactive visual scenes
- Collaborative control interface for attendees
- Audio-linked motion and color systems

## Outcome
The event demonstrated how lightweight tools can support large-scale collaborative digital experiences.
