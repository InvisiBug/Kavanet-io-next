# Local STEM Workshops

![Local STEM Workshops placeholder image](/placeholders/local-stem-workshops.svg)

## Overview
A collection of workshop plans and project kits for teaching software and electronics in small group settings.

## Program Structure
1. Intro concept session
2. Guided build time
3. Reflection and demo walkthrough

## Reusable Assets
- Session decks
- Build prompts
- Mentor notes

## Results
Participants completed practical mini projects and gained confidence in technical problem-solving.
