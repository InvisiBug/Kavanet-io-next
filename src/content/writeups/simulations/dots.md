Each dot starts with a position and direction. As the simulation "ticks" the direction is added to the position to calculate the new position.
When another dot is within a given distance a line is drawn between them, the intensity of which is governed by the distance.

There are a few "disruptor" dots (which are larger) that are bouncing around.
When they are within a given distance or a regular dot a weak "gravitational" force is applied to the regular dot, pulling it towards the "disruptor" dot
This way the simulation has some measure of chaos to keep things from finding a stead state
