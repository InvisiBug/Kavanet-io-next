The rockets objective is to reach the target (the moon) while avoiding obstacles and staying within the canvas.

Initially, most rockets will fail because their movements are completely random.

However, after each generation:
- Rockets that get closer to the target are considered more successful.
- More successful rockets have a greater chance of reproducing.
- Their movement sequences are combined to create new rockets.
- Small random mutations introduce new movements.
Over time, the population should learn to navigate towards the target.
