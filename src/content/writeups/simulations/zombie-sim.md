So one day I was browsing youtube and came across [this video about slime simulations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X-iSQQgOd1A) where the author created an ant colony simulation.

## Classes
* Human
  * They start at a location and head out to find food & bring it back
* Zombie
  * They chase the humans
* Hunters
  * They chase the zombies

## How the sim works
As the human walks around they lay down some pheremons, red for if the human has no food (and is looking for food) and green for if the human has food (and is looking for home)
