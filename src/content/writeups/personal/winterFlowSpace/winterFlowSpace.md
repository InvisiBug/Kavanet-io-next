I use my local puregym's studio as my training space for staff during the winter as the space is free to use by anyone when there isn't a class running. They have app that shows when classes are running but they only show when the class starts and how long it runs for. I wanted to make something that would make easy to see when the studio was free at a glance

The app alows you to select any puregym in the uk and can show you the current number of occupants, useful to avoid the busy times of day :)
